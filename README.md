## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Secure life general hospital under the health sector offers health services across different departments and doing diognosis to a number of patients of all genders with payments backed by insurance and is currently down to assess the challenges in business performance. 
My analysis should help management answer the following questions:
1. How many patient visits has the hospital recorded?
2. Which departments receive the highest number of patients?
3. Which departments generate the highest total bills?
4. What is the average length of stay for patients across different departments?
5. What proportion of patient bills has been paid, remains pending, or has been partially paid?
6. How much has the hospital billed, and how much remains outstanding?
7. How does insurance coverage vary across departments and diagnoses?
8. Which age groups account for the highest number of patient visits?
9. How do admissions and billing amounts change over time?
10. Which areas may require management attention based on the analysis?
As a result the major business issues am trying to address include a drop in admissions in the period 2024-2025, outstanding medical bills and variations in insurance coverage across its departments as evidenced in the hospital data set.

## RECOMMENDATIONS.
Our analysis is within scope but management will need to put more attention to the following based on the analysis and when put into proper and timely consideration Secure Life General Hospital will notice financial and operational positive changes.
1. Partnering with insurance providers
With the highest number of patients being age group 65&above, the hospital should consider partnering more with insurance companies to have these patients enroll on coverage to reduce on the pilling bills and have atractive packages for them.

2. Data quality. There is noticable missing data of 12 patients and use proper billing status.

3. Focus on High-Volume Departments.
General Dept with 252 patient we can reduce waiting time, increase number of specialists to handle the diagnosis and this brings in the most patients,Emergency-190 patients  we should use fast triage protocol to reduce 4.8 days average stay and always check if you are under-utilized with more marketing camps.

4. Revenue Collection challenge
-The partially paid is only 17% so we can do rigorous demand strategies like SMS, calls to ensure the recovery is done to increase the percentage of paid status.
-Insurance average coverage is 75% however we still have bills pending so timely follow up should be done to ensure the insurance pays to reduce the total bill unpaid.
-The hospital should get more staff dedicated to do timely follow-ups on all bills for admissions atleast every 4 days to minimise bill pile up and this will improve the revenue collection and general performance.
